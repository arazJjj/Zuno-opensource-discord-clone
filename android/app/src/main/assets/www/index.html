Mr. Luke's

Teachings in Spelling
and Technology

An original fan game inspired by 90s edutainment horror. All art & sound made in code.

How to Play

  • Find Mr. Luke's 7 iPads in the classrooms. Each one gives you a 3-question quiz.
  • Get one wrong and Mr. Luke starts chasing you. Every wrong answer makes him faster. Don't get caught!
  • With all 7 iPads, reach 4 exits around the school. Each exit closes behind you and things get... redder.
  • Principal Grant sends runners in the halls to detention. Skippy won't let you pass until you do 5 rope jumps. Broom Bot sweeps you away. Byte has a gift.
  • Items: ZAP energy drink (stamina), Quiet Bell (lures Mr. Luke), Hall Pass (escape detention / Grant ignores you).

PC: WASD move, mouse look, Shift run, E / click use, Q / right-click item, 1-3 pick item, M map, Esc pause.
Touch: left stick move, drag right side to look, RUN toggles running, USE picks up, ITEM uses selected item (tap a slot to select).
